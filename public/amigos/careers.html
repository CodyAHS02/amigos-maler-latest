FAQ

Everything you might ask.

How do preservation contracts work?+
You engage us on a monthly or annual basis. We inspect, plan and quietly execute the work required to keep your property performing at its best.
Do you work across all of Switzerland?+
Yes — we serve clients in Zürich, Geneva, Basel, Lugano and the surrounding cantons, with local teams in each region.
Can I cancel or change my plan?+
Plans renew monthly and can be adjusted or paused with 30 days' notice.
Are your prices in the calculator binding?+
The AI estimate is indicative, typically within ±8% of a formal quote. A binding quotation follows an on-site visit.
Do you handle heritage-listed properties?+
Extensively. We hold cantonal certifications for heritage restoration in Zürich, Bern and Basel-Stadt.
